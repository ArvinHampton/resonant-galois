Structural ternary / companion shape alone does **not** force square discriminant.

**Base M (5×5)**  
χ = x⁵ + 3x³ - 3x² - 4889  
Irreducible, contains 3-cycles, Gal = S₅ (disc not square).

**Base T₆ (6×6)**  
χ = x⁶ + 3x⁴ - 3x² - 4889  
Irreducible, contains 3-related cycle types, Gal ≈ S₄ × C₂ (disc not square).

These matrices already encode the order-3 / ternary flavour used throughout the successful constructions. Their existence shows that any axiom list claiming to force even monodromy from the structural shape must be strictly stronger than the templates that produced the experimental A₅ / A₆ hits.
