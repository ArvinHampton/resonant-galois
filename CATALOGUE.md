# Catalogues

## Early matrix-template catalogues (historical)

### A₅ (degree 5)
36 unique monic irreducible polynomials over ℚ with Galois group A₅ obtained from:
- dense lattice search over model/ternary integers
- systematic deformation of the structural 5×5 template M

All pass: irreducibility, square discriminant, presence of 3-cycles, computational confirmation of Gal = A₅.

### A₆ (degree 6)
4 unique monic irreducible polynomials over ℚ with Galois group A₆ obtained from the direct 6×6 enlargement T₆ of the successful template.

Frozen list (representative forms):

- x⁶ − 3x⁴ + 9x² + 18x + 9   (disc = 75816²)
- x⁶ − 3x⁴ + 9x² − 18x + 9   (disc = 75816²)
- x⁶ + x⁵ − 3x⁴ + 4x² − 9    (disc = 47967²)
- x⁶ − x⁵ − 3x⁴ + 4x² − 9    (disc = 47967²)

Cycle types include (5,1), (3,3), (3,1³). Coefficients lie on the pure ternary lattice.
