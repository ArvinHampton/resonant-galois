## Precise statement to prove

### Setup (axiom side)

Let \(\mathbf{HQCC}\) denote a fixed finite list of purely mathematical data drawn from the resonant model, for example some subset of:

1. **Ternary dynamics** — a specified family of maps on \(\mathbb{Z}\) or \(\mathbb{Q}\) with residue classes mod 3 (e.g. branches of type \(n\mapsto n/3\), \(3n\pm1\), …), stated as functions, not as physical clocks;  
2. **Order-3 / lattice generators** — a specified finitely generated monoid or ring of integers (e.g. generated by \(\{3,61,80,243,539,\ldots\}\)) defined by arithmetic generators only;  
3. **Matrix templates** — a specified polynomial family of matrices or charpolys (e.g. \(T(a,\ldots,f)\)) whose entries are constrained by \(\mathbf{HQCC}\);  
4. **Optional geometric data** — a branched cover or local system whose branch labelling is functorially tied to (1)–(3).

**Contamination constraint:** no SI periods (e.g. 539.9 s), detector claims, or unproved dynamical “539-step” laws may appear as hypotheses. Integers such as \(539\) may appear only as elements of \(\mathbb{Z}\).

### Conclusion (Galois side)

There exists a nonempty class \(\mathcal{C}=\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{HQCC})\) of algebraic objects (polynomials, covers, or Galois representations) such that:

1. **Membership in \(\mathcal{C}\) is defined only from \(\mathbf{HQCC}\)** — no extra pure-even ansatz, no “disc □ gate” imported by hand, no classical BJ even locus assumed a priori;  
2. For every \(X\in\mathcal{C}\), the arithmetic monodromy / Galois group \(G_X\) satisfies
   \[
   G_X \supseteq A_{n(X)}
   \quad\text{or}\quad
   G_X = A_{n(X)}
   \]
   for an integer \(n(X)\ge 5\) determined by \(\mathbf{HQCC}\);  
3. \(\mathcal{C}\) is **infinite** (or at least unbounded height) in a sense fixed with \(\mathbf{HQCC}\) (so the result is not a finite list of examples).

### Equivalent formulations

| # | Formulation |
|---|-------------|
| E1 | \(\operatorname{sgn}\circ\rho\equiv 1\) (or equals a fixed HQCC character) for all monodromy representations \(\rho\) arising from \(\mathcal{C}\) |
| E2 | \(\operatorname{disc}(\chi_X)\) is a square for all charpolys of objects in \(\mathcal{C}\), **identically** on the parameter ring of \(\mathcal{C}\), and 3-cycles appear so that \(G_X=A_n\) |
| E3 | A single HQCC-native cover has geometric monodromy \(A_n\) **and** recovers the resonant lattice as Hilbert specialisations without an external evenness ansatz |

Any one of E1–E3, proved under the contamination constraint, is a necessity theorem.

**Target:** A quadratic character \(\chi\) built from ternary / HQCC invariants with

## Obstructions (current)

1. **Evenness is classical** wherever it is identical (pure-even, B-family, Mestre from even seed).  
2. **Templates** with ternary entries realise **odd** monodromy (base \(M\)).  
3. **Rigid \(\varphi/\mathbb{Q}\)** has permanent factor 5 in fibre discs when irreducible.  
4. **Geometric multi-\(k\)** via \(P(q,w)=0\): genus \(g=1\) (ordinary accounting) — no rational param.  
5. **Contamination:** physical / dynamical HQCC claims cannot be smuggled in as proof hypotheses (`CONTAMINATION_BOUNDARY.md`).
