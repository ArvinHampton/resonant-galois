# HQCC seed — clarified definition

## Definition

An **HQCC seed** is a Bring–Jerrard polynomial

\[
s(x) = x^5 + \alpha x + \beta \in \mathbb{Z}[x]
\]

satisfying **all three** of the following.

### 1. Lattice condition

The coefficients \(\alpha,\beta\) are drawn from the **HQCC / resonant lattice**
(or simple integer linear combinations of its elements).

The lattice is generated by the model numbers

\[
\{3,\,9,\,27,\,61,\,80,\,243,\,539,\,\ldots\}
\]

(powers of 3, the puncture value 61, the flux ratio 80, the period 539, and
close neighbours arising from branch evaluations and short sums/products).

### 2. Arithmetic condition

\[
\operatorname{disc}(s)\ \text{is a perfect square}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\mathrm{Gal}(s/\mathbb{Q})=A_5.
\]

(Equivalently: irr + disc² + Frobenius type \((3,1,1)\), by the operational
criterion for degree 5.)

### 3. Homogenisation consequence

The one-parameter family

\[
f_t(x) = x^5 + \alpha\, t^4 x + \beta\, t^5
\]

obeys the identity

\[
\operatorname{disc}(f_t) = t^{20}\cdot\operatorname{disc}(s)
\]

and is therefore **even** for every \(t\neq 0\). Model integers may be
substituted for \(t\) and systematically produce further \(A_5\) polynomials
(when \(f_t\) remains irreducible — empirically the generic case for known seeds).

## Flagship example

\[
s(x) = x^5 - 55x + 88,
\qquad
88 = 61 + 27 = 61 + 3^3.
\]

| Condition | Status |
|-----------|--------|
| Lattice | Satisfied (61 and \(3^3\); \(\alpha=-55\) on the strict HQCC lattice) |
| Arithmetic | Square disc, \(\mathrm{Gal}=A_5\) |
| Homogenisation | \(x^5 - 55 t^4 x + 88 t^5\) proved even; tested model specialisations give \(A_5\) |

Other documented seeds (same definition): \((\alpha,\beta)\in\{(-55,\pm88),\,(95,\pm76),\,(95,\pm532),\,(-100,\pm400),\,(124,\pm496),\,\ldots\}\)
— see `HQCC_STRICT.md`. Classical \((20,\pm16)\) is a BJ seed with the same
homogenisation theorem but is **not** required to be called HQCC-primary.

## What is **not** required for the present definition

1. The seed need **not** arise from a geometric cover or from branch-cycle data
   of the HQCC map.
2. The coefficients need **not** be produced by a closed-form expression
   involving the resonant root \(\xi=2\cos(2\pi/539.9)\).
3. Membership in the full unrestricted \(T_5\) template **outside** the BJ-embed
   subclass is **not** required.

---

## Nature of the definition

The definition is deliberately **arithmetic**:

> An HQCC seed is a **lattice-point Bring–Jerrard polynomial** that already
> carries \(A_5\) and therefore generates an **infinite proved-even family**
> under homogenisation.

The **geometric** (branch-cycle) lift remains a **separate, still-open** problem.
See `THEOREMS.md` (programme position).
