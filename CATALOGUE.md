36 unique monic irreducible polynomials over ℚ with Galois group A₅ obtained from:
- dense lattice search over model/ternary integers
- systematic deformation of the structural 5×5 template M

All pass: irreducibility, square discriminant, presence of 3-cycles, computational confirmation of Gal = A₅.
