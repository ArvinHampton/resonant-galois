# Implications for Resonant Algebra

## Generative role of the ternary lattice

The same numerical lattice that appears in the HQCC / 9 Maths arithmetic (powers of 3, the integers 61, 80, 243, 539, short combinations thereof) systematically produces:

- irreducible quintics with Galois group A₅,
- pure-even families organised by a single rational invariant k,
- multi-seed and multi-k pure-even paths over ℚ.

This demonstrates that the ternary structure is arithmetically fertile for constructive Inverse Galois theory. It supplies an explicit, reproducible source of alternating groups without random search.

## What has been shown

- The lattice is a practical generator of pure-even A₅ polynomials.
- The ratio k = β/α organises an infinite supply of such polynomials into coherent 1-parameter families.
- Cross-ratio paths in the (m,k)-plane yield multi-k pure-even families while remaining inside the arithmetic of the lattice.

## What has not been shown

- A theorem that every (or a dense set of) objects built from the resonant axioms must have alternating monodromy.
- A geometric cover whose branch data are labelled by the HQCC map and whose arithmetic specialisations recover the lattice seeds.
- Any empirical link between these arithmetic constructions and physical observables.

## Recommended use

Treat the pure-even multi-k theory as a self-contained contribution to resonant algebra and constructive Galois theory. Further geometric or physical claims should be advanced only when supported by independent theorems or data.
